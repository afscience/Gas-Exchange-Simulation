How it works: Deoxygenated blood (carrying CO₂ from body cells) enters the capillary on the left. Because O₂ concentration is higher inside the alveolus, oxygen diffuses across the thin membrane into red blood cells. At the same time, CO₂ diffuses from the blood into the alveolus to be exhaled.
